Dredge Daily Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Data Ownership and Storage

Dredge Daily runs locally on the customer's own Windows computers. All reports, spreadsheets, PDFs, equipment logs, and safety records stay on the customer's local drives and the customer's own connected cloud storage account. No third-party application server collects, stores, sells, or shares customer data.

Google Drive and Gmail Access

When a user connects a Google account inside Dredge Daily, the app uses Google Drive strictly to store and sync active contract reports and documents between field sites and the main office computer, and uses Gmail strictly to send and read mail for accounts the user explicitly links. OAuth tokens are encrypted locally on the user's computer.
